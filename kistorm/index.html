HOW TO PLAY

You're always locked on to your opponent. Forward flies straight at them, left/right circles around them.
Punch up close for a 5-hit combo (the last hit sends them flying). Press it from far away to rush in.
Ki blast fires fast homing shots. Beam costs 40 ki. If two beams meet you get a BEAM CLASH, so mash any attack button!
Charge refills ki. Guard blocks most damage. Vanish (25 ki) teleports you behind them, and it works even while you're being comboed.
With 80+ ki, Ascend to transform: faster and hits harder for 30 s.

Keyboard: WASD · Space/Shift up/down · J punch · K blast · L beam · C charge · Q guard · E vanish · T ascend
Touch: left stick to move, buttons on the right.
